Recommended Channel Decision Pages, Nurture Emails

MANDATORY FILMING STANDARD

  • • Orientation: Check your final feed layouts. Use Vertical (9:16) for social platform posting, or Horizontal (16:9) for website homepages.
  • • Audio Rules: Find a silent indoor room away from foot traffic or wind. Stand 3 feet away from the subject for clear mic capture.
  • • Subject Framing: Lock the camera on a flat surface or tripod at eye level. Instruct the speaker to look slightly to the side of the camera lens, directly at you, rather than down the lens.
  • • Speech Structure: "Before joining, we struggled with [Obstacle]. Since starting, [Outcome Metric] happened."
  • • Shot List: Upload 1 long, uninterrupted talking clip (Interview) + 3-4 separate action clips (B-Roll) representing their statements.

Objective 5

Virtual Campus & Facility Tours

A guided tour of your classrooms, playgrounds, and safety features. This helps prospective families feel comfortable with the environment before they ever step foot on campus.

Recommended Channel Hero Header, Google Profile

MANDATORY FILMING STANDARD

  • • Orientation: Select based on distribution preferences (Vertical for reels, Horizontal for web embeds).
  • • Audio Rules: Speak loudly and clearly. Walk and talk in empty, quiet rooms, avoiding high-volume student hallways.
  • • Intro Clip: Stand by the building sign. Film yourself in selfie mode for 5 seconds saying: "Welcome to our campus tour!"
  • • B-Roll Integration: For every room you walk into, switch the camera around and take three steady 5-second b-roll shots of clean desks, security locks, or layout details.
